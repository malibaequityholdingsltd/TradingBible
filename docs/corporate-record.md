### 1.1 Name discrepancy — action required

The certificate of incorporation and the Delaware certified copy both print:

> **TRADINGBIBLE HOLDINGS. INC.**

The company’s public and internal materials generally use:

> **TradingBible Holdings, Inc.**

That is a punctuation difference, but it is still a difference between the filed name and the name used externally. Until Delaware accepts a correction, the filed name controls for government filings, bank/KYC matching, service of process and execution records.

**Required action:** decide whether to keep the filed form or correct it. If the company wants the comma form, an authorized officer should file a Delaware certificate of correction (with the current state fee), then update the registered agent, bank, tax and payment records after acceptance. Do not describe the comma form as the “filed name” before that happens. An execution copy is prepared at `docs/corporate/certificate-of-correction.md`; it is **not filed** and must be reviewed against the Division’s current form before signature.

The certificate also contains an internal date line reading “28th day of October, A.D. 2025”, while the Delaware filing stamp, certified copy and good-standing certificate all state **28 October 2026**. Treat **28 October 2026** as the formation date. Include the date-line inconsistency in the correction review.
