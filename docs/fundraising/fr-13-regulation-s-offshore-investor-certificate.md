## 2. Non-U.S. person certification

The investor certifies that it is not a “U.S. person” within the meaning of Rule 902(k) of Regulation S. A U.S. person includes, among other things:

- a natural person resident in the United States;
- a partnership or corporation organized or incorporated under U.S. law;
- an estate of which any executor or administrator is a U.S. person;
- a trust of which any trustee is a U.S. person;
- an agency or branch of a foreign entity located in the United States;
- a non-discretionary account held by a U.S. person;
- a discretionary account held by a dealer or fiduciary organized in the United States; and
- an entity organized under foreign law that is owned by U.S. persons and formed for the specific purpose of investing in the securities.

## 3. Offshore transaction certification

The investor certifies that:

1. the offer and sale of the securities occurred outside the United States;
2. the investor was outside the United States at the time the offer was received and the sale was made;
3. the investor did not receive the offer in the United States;
4. no directed selling efforts were made by the company or any person acting on its behalf in the United States;
5. the investor is not acquiring the securities for the account or benefit of a U.S. person;
6. the investor will not resell the securities in the United States or to a U.S. person unless the securities are registered or an exemption is available; and
7. the investor will comply with the laws of its own jurisdiction, including any registration, filing, tax or exchange-control requirement.

## 4. Resale restrictions

The investor understands that:

- the securities have not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended;
- the securities are “restricted securities” and may not be resold unless registered or an exemption is available;
- the company may require an opinion of counsel, a Regulation S certificate or other documentation before permitting a transfer;
- no public market exists for the securities, and none is expected to develop;
- the company may place a legend on the securities and the company’s records; and
- the investor bears the economic risk of the investment for an indefinite period.
