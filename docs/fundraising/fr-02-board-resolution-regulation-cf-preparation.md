> **Status.** Execution copy for board adoption. It authorizes preparation only and does not authorize an offer, sale, solicitation or receipt of funds. A Regulation CF offering may not begin until the SEC qualifies the offering statement and the registered intermediary opens the campaign.

## 1. Recitals

**A.** TradingBible Holdings, Inc. is a corporation organized under the laws of the State of [Delaware] and the operator of the TradingBible platform. The jurisdiction and good-standing status must be confirmed from company records before this resolution is relied upon.

**B.** The board is considering whether a future Regulation CF offering through a FINRA-registered funding portal or broker-dealer could broaden access to the company’s financing while preserving the company’s private-placement options.

**C.** Regulation CF requires, among other things, an offering statement on Form C, SEC qualification, an intermediary, investor-limit controls, escrow arrangements and ongoing reporting.

**D.** The board has not selected a portal, approved an offering statement, authorized a campaign or determined the amount, price, valuation or security to be offered.

**E.** The board wishes to authorize preparation and diligence only, while expressly prohibiting any public offer or solicitation until qualification.

## 2. Authorization to prepare

**RESOLVED**, that the company is authorized to evaluate and engage one or more FINRA-registered funding portals or broker-dealers to explore a possible Regulation CF offering, subject to a written engagement letter approved by the chief executive officer and counsel.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company may prepare a working Form C offering statement, financial statements, risk factors, use-of-proceeds schedule, management and ownership disclosures, and campaign materials for counsel and portal review.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company may establish a dedicated data room and engagement record for the potential offering, provided that no working version is published, filed or distributed to the public without counsel approval.

## 3. No offer before qualification

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall not offer or sell securities, accept subscriptions, accept funds, publish a campaign page, launch a “reserve your allocation” feature, or describe the offering as open, live, approved or available until:

1. the SEC has qualified the offering statement;
2. the registered intermediary has opened the campaign;
3. the company has confirmed the investor-limit and escrow controls;
4. the company has approved the final campaign materials; and
5. counsel has confirmed in writing that the offering may commence.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that a working Form C, a portal application, a portal due-diligence request or an internal preparation document is not an offer and shall not be presented as one.

## 4. Testing-the-waters and pre-launch communications

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall not engage in any testing-the-waters communication, public education campaign, social-media post, press interview, webinar or investor presentation about the potential Regulation CF offering unless counsel has confirmed that the communication is permitted and the exact script or materials have been approved in advance.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any permitted pre-launch communication must:

1. identify the company;
2. state clearly that no offer is being made and that any offer will be made only through the registered intermediary after qualification;
3. avoid any statement about the amount, price, valuation, allocation, expected return or likelihood of success;
4. avoid directing the recipient to send money or provide payment details; and
5. be retained in the communications register.

## 5. Portal selection

**RESOLVED**, that the chief executive officer and counsel shall evaluate each potential intermediary on at least the following criteria:

| Criterion | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| FINRA registration | Current registration and CRD number verified |
| Regulation CF experience | Completed campaigns, disclosures and investor complaints reviewed |
| Escrow and investor limits | Written process and system controls confirmed |
| Disclosure review | Counsel and compliance review workflow documented |
| Ongoing reporting support | Annual and current report process defined |
| Fees | All fees, expenses and conflicts disclosed in writing |
| Data and security | Access controls, retention and incident terms reviewed |
| Communications | Rules for issuer statements and social media documented |
| Termination | Exit, transfer and records-return terms documented |

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no engagement letter may be signed until counsel has reviewed the intermediary’s conflicts, compensation, indemnity and data-processing terms.

## 6. Preparation budget

**RESOLVED**, that the company may spend up to **US$[__________]** on legal, accounting, audit, portal, escrow and filing costs associated with preparing a possible Regulation CF offering, subject to the approval of the chief executive officer and the board’s finance oversight.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no preparation expense may be charged to investor funds or treated as a commitment to complete the offering.

## 7. Financial statements and disclosures

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall prepare the financial statements and disclosures required by Regulation CF in consultation with counsel and an independent accountant, and shall not use unaudited management figures as if they were audited or reviewed.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company shall disclose:

1. the nature and stage of its business;
2. the risks that could cause the business to fail;
3. the use of proceeds and the assumptions behind the budget;
4. the company’s ownership, management and related-party relationships;
5. any outstanding debt, SAFEs, options or other convertible instruments;
6. any legal, regulatory or tax proceedings that could be material; and
7. the limited liquidity and resale restrictions applicable to the securities.

## 8. Investor limits and escrow

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall not accept an investment that exceeds the applicable Regulation CF investment limit for the investor, and shall rely on the registered intermediary’s limit calculation and eligibility process, subject to the company’s own review.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all investor funds shall be held in escrow or in the intermediary’s compliant arrangement until the offering reaches its minimum target and closes, and that the company shall not receive or use investor funds before the closing conditions are satisfied.

## 9. Communications and records

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall adopt a written Regulation CF communications policy before any permitted pre-launch communication, and shall maintain a register of:

1. every communication about the potential offering;
2. the date, audience, channel and exact content;
3. the person who approved it;
4. any correction, retraction or complaint; and
5. the legal basis for the communication.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no employee, contractor or agent may communicate about the offering outside the approved policy, and that any unauthorized communication must be reported to counsel immediately.

## 10. Ongoing reporting

**RESOLVED**, that if the offering is completed, the company shall prepare for the ongoing reporting obligations applicable to Regulation CF issuers, including annual reports, current reports and termination filings, and shall appoint a named reporting owner.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company shall not treat the completion of the campaign as the end of the compliance obligation.

## 11. No other authorization

**RESOLVED**, that nothing in this resolution authorizes:

1. a public offering or sale of securities;
2. the acceptance of funds;
3. a token sale or digital-asset distribution;
4. a fund, brokerage, advisory, exchange or custodial activity; or
5. any statement that the SEC, FINRA, a portal or any regulator has approved the company or the offering.

## 12. Effective date and certification

**RESOLVED**, that this resolution is effective when adopted by the board and certified by the secretary.
