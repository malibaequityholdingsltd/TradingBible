> **Status.** Execution copy for adoption by the board of directors. It becomes effective when the board adopts it and the secretary certifies the adopted text. The offering terms are set out in the board-approved schedules; no offer may be made until those schedules are completed and the resolution is adopted.

## 1. Recitals

**A.** TradingBible Holdings, Inc. is a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware. The certificate of incorporation and a current good-standing certificate are the controlling records for this recital.

**B.** The board of directors has reviewed a proposed private placement of simple agreements for future equity to support the company’s product, operating and launch requirements.

**C.** The board intends the offering to be conducted in reliance on Rule 506(b) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, for offers and sales to accredited U.S. investors, and in reliance on Regulation S for offers and sales to eligible non-U.S. investors outside the United States.

**D.** The board intends that the offering will not involve general solicitation or general advertising, and that each investor will complete the company’s eligibility, bad-actor and risk documentation before signing.

**E.** The board has not authorized a public offering, an initial public offering, a token sale, a fund vehicle or any other regulated activity by this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the minimum investment for any single investor is **US$[__________]**, unless the chief executive officer and counsel approve a lower amount in writing for a particular investor.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the valuation cap for the offering is **US$[__________]** on a post-money basis, that no discount applies, and that the pro rata rights, if any, are limited to investors purchasing at least **US$[__________]**.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the offering period begins on the date this resolution is adopted and ends twelve months later, unless extended by the board in writing. No extension may be used to increase the Maximum Offering Amount without a further board resolution.

## 4. Authority of officers

**RESOLVED**, that the chief executive officer, and each other officer expressly designated by the board in writing, is authorized, on behalf of the company, to:

1. determine which prospective investors have a pre-existing substantive relationship with the company and are eligible to receive the offering documents;
2. conduct one-to-one discussions with prospective investors, subject to the communications policy in this resolution;
3. determine that each U.S. investor is an accredited investor on the basis of the completed questionnaire and any additional verification the officer considers appropriate;
4. determine that each non-U.S. investor is eligible under Regulation S on the basis of the completed certificate and any additional diligence the officer considers appropriate;
5. accept subscriptions, execute SAFEs and issue the instruments at one or more closings;
6. establish and maintain the SAFE register and update the cap table after each closing;
7. make all filings and notices required by Rule 503 of Regulation D and applicable state law;
8. engage counsel, auditors, escrow agents and other advisers as necessary; and
9. take all further actions, execute all further documents and pay all fees that are necessary or advisable to carry out this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any authority granted to an officer is limited by the Maximum Offering Amount, the approved term sheet, the use-of-proceeds budget and the conditions in this resolution. An officer may not increase the Maximum Offering Amount, change the valuation cap, waive a closing condition or approve a related-party investment without a further board resolution.

## 5. Use of proceeds

**RESOLVED**, that the proceeds of the offering shall be used only for the categories and amounts approved in the budget attached as Schedule A, and that any reallocation above the amount or percentage recorded in Schedule A requires board approval.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company shall maintain the proceeds in a segregated account or a separately identified ledger until they are applied to the approved budget, and that the chief executive officer shall report the use of proceeds to the board at least quarterly.

## 6. No general solicitation

**RESOLVED**, that the offering shall be conducted without general solicitation or general advertising within the meaning of Rule 502(c) of Regulation D. Without limiting the foregoing, the company shall not:

1. publish or distribute any advertisement, article, notice or other communication about the offering in any newspaper, magazine, website, social-media platform, television, radio, email list or similar medium;
2. conduct a public webinar, public livestream, public “ask me anything” session or public event at which the offering is described as an investment opportunity;
3. use the TradingBible website, member application, TradingBible TV, public investor-relations pages or public social accounts to announce or promote the offering;
4. send an offering communication to a person who has not been individually identified and cleared by an authorized officer; or
5. permit any investor, employee, contractor or agent to forward the offering package to an unrestricted audience.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company may maintain a private investor-relations subscription list for company updates, but that subscription does not constitute an offer, a solicitation or a pre-existing substantive relationship for purposes of the offering.

## 7. Investor eligibility and bad-actor diligence

**RESOLVED**, that before any sale, the company shall obtain from each investor:

1. a completed accredited-investor questionnaire or Regulation S certificate, as applicable;
2. a completed bad-actor questionnaire covering the investor and its covered persons;
3. a completed risk disclosure and acknowledgment;
4. sufficient information to verify identity, source of funds and sanctions status; and
5. any tax form required by law, including the current IRS Form W-9, W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E, as applicable.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the company shall complete Rule 506(d) bad-actor diligence on the company and its covered persons before the first sale, and shall not make an offer or sale if a disqualifying event exists unless counsel confirms that a reasonable-care exception applies and the required disclosure is made.

## 8. Form D and state notices

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall file a Form D with the Securities and Exchange Commission no later than 15 calendar days after the first sale of securities in the offering, and shall make any required state notice filings and pay any required fees.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the filing of a Form D does not constitute an approval of the offering by the SEC or any state, and that the company shall not describe a Form D filing as an approval, clearance or endorsement.

## 9. Related-party participation

**RESOLVED**, that any director, officer, founder, employee or affiliate of the company who wishes to purchase SAFEs in the offering may do so only on the same terms as unrelated investors, with full written disclosure of the relationship, and only after the disinterested directors approve the participation.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no insider may receive material non-public information about other investors, allocation, pricing or closing that is not available to all investors in the offering.

## 10. Closing conditions

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall not accept funds or issue a SAFE unless:

1. the investor’s eligibility and bad-actor documentation is complete;
2. the investor has executed the SAFE through DocuSign or another approved electronic-signature process;
3. the company has countersigned the SAFE;
4. the funds have cleared in the company’s approved account;
5. the SAFE register and cap table have been updated; and
6. counsel has confirmed that the closing does not exceed the Maximum Offering Amount or violate the approved term sheet.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the chief executive officer may waive a condition only if the waiver is in writing, is approved by counsel and does not violate securities law or the board-approved term sheet.

## 11. Records and reports

**RESOLVED**, that the company shall maintain complete records of:

1. each prospective investor contacted and the basis for the pre-existing relationship;
2. each offering document sent and the date sent;
3. each questionnaire, certificate and acknowledgment received;
4. each DocuSign envelope, certificate of completion and completed PDF;
5. each subscription, closing, wire, SAFE issuance and cap-table update;
6. each Form D and state filing; and
7. each communication that could constitute an offering communication.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the chief executive officer shall report to the board at least monthly on the status of the offering, the funds received, the securities issued and any compliance exception.

## 12. No other authorization

**RESOLVED**, that nothing in this resolution authorizes:

1. a public offering, an initial public offering or a listing of the company’s securities;
2. a token sale, token issuance, staking, governance, migration or chain launch;
3. the formation of a fund, the acceptance of fund capital or the marketing of any fund;
4. brokerage, advisory, exchange or custodial activity; or
5. any communication that would constitute general solicitation.

## 13. Effective date and certification

**RESOLVED**, that this resolution is effective when adopted by the board and certified by the secretary, and that the secretary is authorized to certify the adopted text to counsel, investors and service providers.
