> **Internal execution policy.** This policy governs fundraising envelopes sent through DocuSign. It does not replace DocuSign’s terms, the company’s electronic-signature policy or counsel’s instructions. A completed envelope is evidence of signature, not a regulatory approval or a completed offering.

## 1. Authorized senders

Only the following persons may prepare or send a fundraising envelope:

| Role | Permission | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Chief executive officer | Prepare, send, void and resend | All fundraising envelopes |
| Designated finance owner | Prepare and send | Only after written delegation |
| Counsel | Review and receive copies | No unilateral send unless authorized |
| Other personnel | None | No access |

The company must maintain a current list of authorized senders and remove access immediately when a person leaves the role.

## 2. Before sending

Before an envelope is created, the sender must verify:

1. the recipient’s legal name, email address and capacity;
2. the recipient’s eligibility certificate and bad-actor questionnaire;
3. the board resolution and approved term sheet;
4. the controlled PDF version and its document number;
5. the recipient’s authority to sign for an entity;
6. the correct signer order and required/optional status;
7. the correct completion fields and anchors; and
8. that the recipient has consented to electronic signature.

## 3. Envelope construction

1. Use only the PDF generated from the controlled document library.
2. Do not edit the PDF outside the controlled build process.
3. Anchor signature, name, capacity and date tabs only to the recipient’s own execution block.
4. Anchor custom text and checkbox tabs only to the recipient’s own completion fields.
5. Do not place a company signature field in an investor block or an investor field in a company block.
6. Set the envelope subject and message from the approved template.
7. Do not include bank details, passwords, one-time codes or private keys in the envelope message.
8. If a document requires a witness or notary, confirm the requirement with counsel before sending.

## 4. Sending

1. Send only to the verified recipient email address.
2. Do not copy an unverified distribution list.
3. Do not use “reply all” or a shared mailbox for investor correspondence.
4. Record the envelope ID, document number, recipient, date and sender in the execution register.
5. If a recipient says they did not receive the envelope, verify their identity before resending.
6. If a recipient’s email changes, void the old envelope and create a new one after verification.

## 5. Completion

When an envelope is completed:

1. download the certificate of completion and the completed PDF;
2. verify that every required field is completed and that no field was completed by the wrong party;
3. confirm that the signer’s name and capacity match the eligibility records;
4. record the completion date and envelope status;
5. store the completed PDF and certificate in the controlled data room;
6. update the SAFE register and cap table, if applicable; and
7. send the completed copy to the investor using the approved completion template.

A DocuSign “completed” status does not prove that funds were received, that a closing occurred or that a filing was made. Those facts require separate evidence.

## 6. Declines, voids and corrections

| Event | Action | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Recipient declines | Confirm reason; do not pressure; update register | Decline notice and correspondence |
| Envelope voided | Record reason and sender; create a new envelope only after review | Void confirmation |
| Incorrect recipient | Void immediately; notify counsel; verify correct recipient | Incident note |
| Incorrect document version | Void immediately; rebuild from controlled source | Version log |
| Incorrect field placement | Void immediately; correct manifest and rebuild | Quality-control note |
| Suspected fraud | Stop the process; notify counsel and the bank; preserve evidence | Incident report |

## 7. Records retention

The company must retain:

- the executed PDF;
- the certificate of completion;
- the envelope history and recipient status;
- the eligibility and bad-actor records;
- the board resolution and approved term sheet;
- the funding and closing records;
- the SAFE register and cap-table update; and
- all related correspondence.

Records must be retained for at least the period required by law, the company’s document-retention policy and the investor’s agreement. The company should retain them longer if a dispute, audit or regulatory inquiry is reasonably possible.

## 8. Access and security

1. DocuSign credentials must be unique to the authorized sender.
2. Multi-factor authentication is required.
3. Shared credentials are prohibited.
4. Access must be removed within one business day of a role change or termination.
5. Envelopes must be stored in the company’s controlled account, not a personal account.
6. The company must review access logs quarterly.

## 9. Quality-control checklist

- [ ] Document number and version match the manifest.
- [ ] Recipient identity and authority verified.
- [ ] Eligibility and bad-actor records complete.
- [ ] Signature and completion fields anchored to the correct block.
- [ ] No bank details in the envelope message.
- [ ] Envelope ID recorded.
- [ ] Completed PDF and certificate downloaded.
- [ ] SAFE register and cap table updated.
- [ ] Investor completion notice sent.
- [ ] Records stored in the controlled data room.
