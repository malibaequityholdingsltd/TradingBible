## 2. The problem

Retail traders often work across disconnected charts, brokers, spreadsheets, journals, courses and social feeds. The result is a fragmented record: the trade is in one system, the reason is in another, the review is in a third and the risk data is rarely connected to the decision. That fragmentation makes it difficult to learn from mistakes, demonstrate a process or improve discipline.

An investment in the company is speculative and involves a high degree of risk. You should be prepared to lose your entire investment. The following risks are material, but they are not the only risks.

### 9.2 No public market

### 9.3 Dilution

Future financings, option grants, warrants, convertible notes or SAFEs may dilute your ownership and reduce the value of your investment. The company may issue securities at a price below the price you pay.

### 9.4 Regulation CF and regulatory risk

The offering is subject to SEC qualification and ongoing reporting. The company may not obtain qualification, may receive SEC comments that delay or change the offering, or may be unable to comply with ongoing obligations. The company is not a licensed broker-dealer, investment adviser, exchange, custodian or fund.

### 9.5 Token, fund, brokerage and card risk

### 9.6 Technology and cybersecurity

### 9.7 AI output

### 9.8 Competition

The trading-software, market-data, education and AI markets are competitive. Larger competitors may have more capital, data, distribution and regulatory resources.

### 9.9 Key personnel

The company depends on a small team. The loss of a key person or an inability to hire qualified staff could harm the business.
