> **Internal preparation workbook.** This is not an official SEC form, a Form C filing or a complete disclosure document. It is a field-by-field schedule for collecting the information that counsel and the registered intermediary will need. Do not file this workbook. Do not describe it as a Form C.

## 1. How to use this workbook

1. Assign an owner for each section.
2. Attach the source document or company record for every answer.
3. Mark unverified facts as **OPEN**.
4. Do not fill a gap with an estimate unless the estimate is labelled and supported.
5. Have counsel and the intermediary review the completed workbook before anyone drafts the Form C.
