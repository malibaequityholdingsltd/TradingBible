> **Drafting note.** This is a company-specific post-money SAFE form prepared for counsel review. It uses widely used post-money SAFE concepts but is not a Y Combinator form and must not be described as one. Bracketed terms must be completed from the board-approved term sheet. The definitive agreement is the executed copy delivered through DocuSign.

## 1. Definitions

For purposes of this Safe, the following terms have the meanings below.

**“Company”** means TradingBible Holdings, Inc., a corporation organized under the laws of the State of [Delaware].

**“Investor”** means the person identified in Schedule A.

**“Purchase Amount”** means the amount set out in Schedule A, as paid in full to the Company.

**“Valuation Cap”** means the post-money valuation set out in Schedule A.

**“Equity Financing”** means a bona fide transaction or series of related transactions in which the Company issues and sells shares of its preferred stock to investors for cash, with the principal purpose of raising capital, and in which the Company’s pre-money valuation is at least the Valuation Cap.

**“Liquidity Event”** means:

1. a closing of a firm-commitment underwritten public offering of the Company’s common stock;
2. a direct listing of the Company’s common stock on a national securities exchange;
3. a merger, consolidation or other business combination in which the Company is not the surviving entity or in which the Company’s stockholders do not hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving entity; or
4. a sale, lease, exclusive license or other disposition of all or substantially all of the Company’s assets.

**“Dissolution Event”** means a voluntary or involuntary liquidation, dissolution, winding up or other Deemed Liquidation Event of the Company that is not a Liquidity Event.

**“Conversion Shares”** means the shares of the Company’s capital stock issued to the Investor upon conversion of this Safe.

**“Safe Price”** means the price per share equal to the Valuation Cap divided by the Company’s fully diluted capitalization immediately before the Equity Financing, as adjusted for stock splits, combinations, dividends and similar events.

**“Pro Rata Rights”** means the right, if any, set out in Schedule A.

## 2. Purchase and issuance

The Investor is purchasing this Safe for the Purchase Amount. The Company is issuing this Safe in reliance on Rule 506(b) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, for accredited U.S. investors, or Regulation S for eligible non-U.S. investors. The Investor is not purchasing a share of stock, a convertible note, a debt instrument or a unit of any fund.

This Safe is a grant of a right to receive equity in the future. It is not secured, does not accrue interest, does not have a maturity date and is not repayable except as expressly provided in this Safe. The Company may never complete an Equity Financing, a Liquidity Event or a Dissolution Event, and the Investor may never receive shares or cash.

## 3. Conversion in an Equity Financing

If an Equity Financing occurs before the termination of this Safe, this Safe will automatically convert into Conversion Shares at the Safe Price, subject to the following:

1. The number of Conversion Shares will equal the Purchase Amount divided by the Safe Price.
2. The Safe Price will be determined on a fully diluted basis, including all outstanding shares, options, warrants, convertible securities and all shares reserved for issuance under the Company’s equity incentive plans, and treating all SAFEs (including this Safe) on an as-converted basis using the same post-money valuation cap methodology.
3. The Company will deliver to the Investor a certificate or book-entry statement for the Conversion Shares, together with a copy of the executed financing documents and a calculation of the Safe Price and the number of Conversion Shares.
4. The Conversion Shares will have the same rights, preferences and privileges as the shares issued to the other investors in the Equity Financing, except that the Investor’s pro rata rights, if any, will be as set out in Schedule A.
5. If the Equity Financing closes at a pre-money valuation below the Valuation Cap, this Safe will not convert at the Safe Price unless the Company and the Investor agree in writing. In that case, the Company will treat the Safe in accordance with the terms approved by the board and disclosed to the Investor, which may include conversion at the actual financing price, a right to participate in the financing or another treatment that does not impair the Investor’s rights under this Safe.

## 4. Liquidity Event

If a Liquidity Event occurs before the termination of this Safe, the Company will, at its option, either:

1. pay the Investor a cash amount equal to the greater of (a) the Purchase Amount and (b) the amount the Investor would have received if this Safe had converted into common stock immediately before the Liquidity Event at the Safe Price; or
2. cause the Investor to receive the same form of consideration and the same per-share amount that the Investor would have received if this Safe had converted into common stock immediately before the Liquidity Event at the Safe Price.

If the consideration in the Liquidity Event is not cash, the Company will use a reasonable valuation method, approved by the board and disclosed to the Investor, to determine the value of the consideration. The Company will pay or deliver the Investor’s portion at the closing of the Liquidity Event, subject to any escrow, holdback or indemnity arrangement that applies to the Company’s stockholders generally.

## 5. Dissolution Event

If a Dissolution Event occurs before the termination of this Safe, the Company will pay the Investor, before any distribution to the holders of common stock, an amount equal to the lesser of (a) the Purchase Amount and (b) the Investor’s pro rata share of the assets legally available for distribution to stockholders, based on the Investor’s as-converted ownership immediately before the Dissolution Event. The Investor will not receive any other distribution in respect of this Safe.

## 6. Pro rata rights

If Schedule A states that the Investor has Pro Rata Rights, then in connection with the Equity Financing, the Investor will have the right, but not the obligation, to purchase its pro rata share of the securities issued in the Equity Financing on the same terms as the other investors. The Investor’s pro rata share will be determined by dividing the number of Conversion Shares by the total number of shares outstanding immediately after the Equity Financing, on a fully diluted basis. The right must be exercised within the period stated in the financing documents and is subject to the Investor remaining an accredited investor or eligible non-U.S. person, as applicable.

10. The Investor is not a sanctioned person and is not owned or controlled by a sanctioned person, and the source of the Purchase Amount is lawful.

## 9. Transfer restrictions

The Investor may not transfer this Safe or any Conversion Shares without the Company’s prior written consent, except by operation of law or to a permitted transferee that agrees in writing to be bound by this Safe. Any permitted transfer must comply with the Securities Act, applicable state law and the Company’s charter and bylaws. The Company may require an opinion of counsel, an eligibility certificate and any other documentation it reasonably requests.

## 10. No rights as stockholder

The Investor has no voting rights, dividend rights, information rights or other rights as a stockholder of the Company by reason of this Safe, except as expressly provided in this Safe or required by law. The Investor has no right to participate in the management of the Company or to consent to any corporate action.

## 11. Tax matters

The Company and the Investor will treat this Safe in a manner consistent with applicable tax law. The Investor is responsible for obtaining its own tax advice. The Company may withhold or report amounts as required by law. Nothing in this Safe creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship between the parties.

## 12. Amendment and waiver

This Safe may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Company and the Investor. No waiver of any provision is effective unless in writing, and no waiver on one occasion is a waiver on any other occasion.

## 13. Notices

All notices under this Safe must be in writing and delivered to the addresses in Schedule A, or by email with confirmation of receipt. A notice by email is effective when sent if sent before 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day, and otherwise on the next business day.

## 14. Governing law and disputes

This Safe is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. The parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion. If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days, either party may bring an action in the state or federal courts located in Delaware, and each party consents to the jurisdiction of those courts. Nothing in this section prevents either party from seeking injunctive relief.

## 15. Miscellaneous

1. This Safe, together with the offering documents and the Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.
2. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in effect.
3. This Safe may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, including DocuSign. An electronic signature has the same effect as an original signature.
4. The headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
5. The Company may issue additional SAFEs, options, warrants or other securities after the date of this Safe, subject to the board-approved cap and the Company’s charter.
6. This Safe does not create any obligation to issue shares in the future except as expressly provided.
