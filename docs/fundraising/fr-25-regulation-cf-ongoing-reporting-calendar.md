## 2. Annual report working file

The Form C-AR working file should include:

- audited or reviewed financial statements, as required;
- management’s discussion of the business and results;
- risk factors and material changes;
- use of proceeds and a comparison to the offering statement;
- related-party transactions;
- management and ownership changes;
- legal, regulatory and tax proceedings;
- investor communications and complaints; and
- counsel and accountant sign-offs.

## 3. Current report triggers

The company should treat the following as potential current-report triggers and confirm each with counsel:

- a material change in the company’s business, management or financial condition;
- a material legal or regulatory proceeding;
- a material default or financing event;
- a change in the offering terms or use of proceeds;
- a material cybersecurity incident;
- a change in the company’s accountants or financial statements;
- a transaction that could affect the issuer’s eligibility; and
- any event the intermediary requires the company to disclose.

## 5. Records and evidence

The company must retain:

1. the qualified offering statement and all amendments;
2. the campaign page and all investor communications;
3. the investor-limit and escrow records;
4. the closing documents and securities register;
5. the annual and current reports, with filing confirmations;
6. the board minutes approving each report;
7. counsel and accountant sign-offs; and
8. the complaint and correction log.

Records should be stored in a controlled data room with access limited to the reporting owner, counsel, finance and the chief executive officer.

## 6. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief executive officer | Approves reports and certifies accuracy |
| Finance owner | Prepares financial statements and use-of-proceeds reconciliation |
| Counsel | Reviews disclosure, filings and current-report triggers |
| Accountant | Reviews or audits financial statements as required |
| Reporting owner | Maintains the calendar, files reports and retains confirmations |
| Intermediary | Coordinates campaign records and investor communications |

## 7. Escalation

Any missed deadline, late filing, material misstatement or regulatory inquiry must be escalated to the chief executive officer and counsel within one business day. The company must not wait for the next scheduled board meeting.
