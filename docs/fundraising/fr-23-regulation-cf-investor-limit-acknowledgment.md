> **Investor acknowledgment.** This document is used only in a qualified Regulation CF offering through a registered intermediary. It does not itself create an investment, an allocation or a binding subscription. The intermediary’s eligibility and limit determination controls.

## 1. Why this acknowledgment is required

Regulation CF limits how much an investor may invest in all Regulation CF offerings during a 12-month period. The limit depends on the investor’s annual income and net worth. The registered intermediary must determine whether an investment is within the limit and may reject or cancel an investment that exceeds it.

The numerical thresholds are adjusted by the SEC from time to time. **Do not rely on this document for the current dollar thresholds.** Use the intermediary’s current calculator and the qualified offering statement.

## 3. Limit certification

The investor certifies that:

1. the income and net-worth figures provided are true, complete and accurate;
2. the investor has reviewed the intermediary’s current Regulation CF investment-limit calculation;
3. the proposed investment, together with all other Regulation CF investments in the 12-month period, does not exceed the applicable limit;
4. the investor will not use borrowed funds or a loan from the company or an affiliate to make the investment without disclosing it to the intermediary;
5. the investor will notify the intermediary immediately if the information changes before the closing; and
6. the investor understands that the intermediary may reject, reduce or cancel the investment if the limit is exceeded or the information cannot be verified.

## 5. Risk acknowledgment

The investor acknowledges that:

- the investment is speculative and illiquid;
- the investor may lose the entire amount invested;
- the securities are restricted and may not be resold unless registered or an exemption is available;
- the company may never complete a liquidity event;
- the investment does not create a fund interest, a brokerage account or a guaranteed return;
- the company is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, exchange, custodian or fund; and
- the investor has reviewed the qualified offering statement and the risk factors.

## 6. Cancellation and cooling-off

The investor understands that:

1. the intermediary’s terms may provide a right to cancel an investment within a specified period;
2. the company may not close the offering until the minimum target and other conditions are satisfied;
3. if the offering is cancelled or does not close, the investor’s funds will be returned in accordance with the intermediary’s escrow terms; and
4. the investor must follow the intermediary’s cancellation process and may not rely on an email to the company as a valid cancellation.
