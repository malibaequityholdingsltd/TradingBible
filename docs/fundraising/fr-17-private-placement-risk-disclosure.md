> **Investor risk disclosure.** Read this document in full before signing a SAFE. An investment in TradingBible Holdings, Inc. involves a high degree of risk. You may lose the entire amount invested. This document does not describe every risk, and the company may discover new risks after the date of this document.

## 1. Early-stage company risk

TradingBible is an early-stage private company. It has a public platform, a product plan and a launch date, but it has not published audited financial statements, a verified revenue history, a valuation approved by an independent adviser or a track record of profitability. The company may not achieve its operating plan, may need more capital than expected, may fail to retain key personnel or may cease operations. A launch date is not a guarantee that any product or feature will launch on time or at all.

## 2. No public market and illiquidity

There is no public market for the SAFE or for any shares issued on conversion. The securities are restricted and may not be resold unless registered or an exemption is available. The company is under no obligation to register the securities, list them on an exchange or facilitate a sale. You should expect to hold the investment indefinitely and to bear the complete loss if the company fails.

## 3. SAFE mechanics

A SAFE is not a share of stock, a convertible note, a secured obligation or a fund interest. It does not pay interest, has no maturity date and is not repayable on demand. Its value depends on a future Equity Financing, Liquidity Event or Dissolution Event. The company may never complete any of those events. If an Equity Financing occurs, the SAFE may convert at a price that is less favorable than you expect, and your ownership may be diluted by future issuances, option pools, warrants, convertible notes or other SAFEs.

## 4. Valuation cap and dilution

The Valuation Cap is a negotiated ceiling, not a guarantee of value. It does not mean the company is worth that amount today, that investors will receive that value or that a future financing will occur at or above the cap. The company’s fully diluted capitalization may change before conversion. A future financing may be priced below the cap, may include a liquidation preference or other terms that reduce the value of the common stock you receive, or may not occur at all.

## 5. Management and key-person risk

The company depends on a small team, including its founder and chief executive officer. The loss of a key person, a dispute among founders or an inability to hire qualified staff could harm the business. The company’s documented team includes an AI research function; an AI system is not a human officer, employee or fiduciary and may produce incomplete or incorrect output.

## 6. Regulatory and licensing risk

The company is not a licensed broker-dealer, investment adviser, exchange, custodian or fund. Its proposed brokerage, fund, card, token and digital-asset initiatives require licences, approvals, banking relationships, custody arrangements and compliance infrastructure that the company does not currently have. Those initiatives may never launch. A launch date, corporate registration, D-U-N-S number or EIN is not a regulatory licence or an approval of any product.

## 7. Token and digital-asset risk

TBC is a proposed digital asset. No TBC token has been issued, no sale is open and no staking, governance, migration or chain function is live. TBC is not equity in the company and does not represent a claim on the company’s assets or revenues. Any future token could be subject to securities, commodities, tax, AML, sanctions and market-conduct laws in multiple jurisdictions. The company may never obtain the approvals required to issue or distribute it.

## 8. Fund and investment-product risk

TradingBible Capital is in formation. It has no fund vehicle, private placement memorandum, auditor, administrator, prime broker, custodian, compliance manual, insurance or track record. No outside fund capital is being accepted. An investment in the company is not an investment in any fund, and it does not give you any interest in a fund or in any fund performance.

## 9. Technology, data and cybersecurity risk

The platform depends on third-party market data, cloud infrastructure, brokers, payment processors, AI providers and other vendors. A vendor outage, data error, cyberattack, credential compromise, software defect or service interruption could harm the business, expose user data, cause regulatory action or damage the company’s reputation. The company’s security controls reduce risk but cannot eliminate it.

## 10. AI and model risk

The company uses AI-generated reviews, coaching and research. AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased or unsuitable for a particular decision. It is not investment advice and should not be relied on as a substitute for independent judgment. Model providers may change their terms, pricing, availability or accuracy, and the company may not be able to replace them on acceptable terms.

## 11. Market and competition risk

The trading-software, market-data, education and AI markets are competitive and change quickly. Larger competitors may have more capital, data, distribution and regulatory resources. The company may not be able to acquire customers at an acceptable cost, retain them, price its products profitably or differentiate its platform.

## 12. Revenue and financial reporting risk

The company has not published audited financial statements in this package. Any revenue, subscriber, margin or growth figure added later may be unaudited, may be calculated on a management basis and may not be comparable to another company’s figures. You should not assume that any historical figure will continue or that any projection will be achieved.

## 13. Tax risk

The tax treatment of a SAFE, its conversion and any future liquidity event is complex and may vary by jurisdiction. The company does not provide tax advice. You are responsible for obtaining your own advice and for any filing, election or payment required in your jurisdiction. A change in tax law could reduce the value of your investment.
