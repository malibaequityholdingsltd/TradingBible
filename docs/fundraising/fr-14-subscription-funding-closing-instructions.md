> **Confidential investor instructions.** Follow each step in order. Do not send funds until you have received a countersigned SAFE and have independently verified the funding instructions. The company will never change bank details by email without a separate call-back to a known number.

## 1. Step-by-step process

### Step 1 — Receive and review the package

You should receive:

- the Confidential Private Placement Summary and Notice (FR-10);
- the Post-Money SAFE (FR-11);
- the Accredited Investor and Bad Actor Questionnaire (FR-12) or the Regulation S Offshore Investor Certificate (FR-13);
- the Private Placement Risk Disclosure and Acknowledgment (FR-17); and
- these instructions.

If any document is missing, stop and contact the company at **legal@tradingbible.app**.

### Step 2 — Complete eligibility

Complete the applicable eligibility certificate and the risk disclosure. Answer every question. If a question does not apply, write “N/A” rather than leaving it blank. The company may request supporting documents, including entity formation documents, beneficial-ownership information, tax forms or source-of-funds evidence.

### Step 3 — Review the SAFE

Review the SAFE, including the Valuation Cap, Purchase Amount, pro rata rights and governing law. Ask the company or its counsel any questions before signing. Do not sign if any term is unclear or if the document does not match the approved term sheet.

### Step 4 — Sign through DocuSign

The company will send the SAFE and the applicable certificate through DocuSign. Complete only the fields assigned to you. The DocuSign envelope will include signature, printed-name, capacity and date fields. The certificate may include additional completion fields.

### Step 5 — Receive the countersigned SAFE

The company will verify your signature, countersign the SAFE and deliver a completed copy. A DocuSign “completed” status is not, by itself, evidence that funds have been received or that the closing has occurred.

### Step 6 — Verify funding instructions independently

The company will deliver funding instructions through a separate secure channel. Before sending funds:

1. call the company at a known number, not a number in the email;
2. confirm the account name, bank, account number and reference;
3. confirm the amount and the currency;
4. confirm that the account is controlled by TradingBible Holdings, Inc.; and
5. record the name of the person who confirmed the instructions.

If you cannot verify the instructions, do not send funds.

### Step 7 — Send funds and notify the company

Send the funds using the verified instructions. Include the investor’s legal name in the reference. Email the company a copy of the remittance advice, but never email full bank details, passwords or private keys.

### Step 8 — Closing

The company will confirm receipt of cleared funds, update the SAFE register and cap table, and issue the executed SAFE. The company may decline a subscription if eligibility, sanctions, source-of-funds or closing conditions are not satisfied.

## 3. Wire fraud warning

Fraudsters impersonate companies and change bank details. The company will not:

- send bank details in an unsolicited email;
- ask you to send funds to a person rather than the company;
- ask you to keep the funding confidential from your bank or adviser;
- ask for your online banking password, one-time code or private key; or
- pressure you to send funds before you have a countersigned SAFE.

If you receive a suspicious message, forward it to **legal@tradingbible.app** and call the company using a known number.

## 4. Tax and regulatory responsibility

The investor is responsible for:

- completing the correct current IRS form (W-9, W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E);
- complying with the laws of the investor’s jurisdiction;
- obtaining independent tax, legal and financial advice; and
- making any required filing or notification in the investor’s jurisdiction.

The company does not provide tax or legal advice.
