# TRADINGBIBLE HOLDINGS, INC. — OFFBOARDING AND ACCESS-REVOCATION PACK

**Controlled template · Version 1.0 · 12 November 2026**

> **Controlled template — local-law review required.** This pack is an operating procedure. It is not a termination notice or legal advice. Follow the notice, consultation, final-pay, works-council and documentation requirements of the employee’s work location. Do not use the same process for a resignation, a redundancy, a performance dismissal and a misconduct termination.

## 1. Decision and legal review

- [ ] Confirm the lawful basis for termination or record the resignation.
- [ ] Confirm the effective date and notice period.
- [ ] Confirm whether consultation, works-council, employee-representative or regulatory notification applies.
- [ ] Confirm whether garden leave, suspension or immediate access revocation is lawful and appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm the final-pay rules, accrued leave, commissions, bonuses and deductions.
- [ ] Confirm the treatment of pension, insurance, equity, options and benefits.
- [ ] Confirm the reference and confirmation-of-employment policy.
- [ ] Record the decision, approver and legal reference in the restricted HR file.
- [ ] Prepare the termination letter or acceptance of resignation using counsel-reviewed language.

## 2. Before the notice meeting

- [ ] Prepare the written notice or separation agreement.
- [ ] Prepare the final-pay calculation.
- [ ] Prepare the benefit and pension discontinuation or transfer instructions.
- [ ] Identify the manager or HR representative who will attend.
- [ ] Arrange a private meeting room or secure video call.
- [ ] Prepare the equipment-return instructions.
- [ ] Prepare the access-revocation plan without alerting the employee prematurely.
- [ ] Confirm who will communicate with the team, customers and partners.
- [ ] Preserve relevant records and litigation-hold material.

## 3. Notice meeting

- [ ] Explain the decision clearly and respectfully.
- [ ] Provide the written notice and the effective date.
- [ ] Explain final pay, benefits, leave, pension, equity and return-of-property.
- [ ] Explain the expected work and handover during notice.
- [ ] Give the employee the opportunity to ask questions.
- [ ] Provide the grievance, appeal or whistleblowing route where applicable.
- [ ] Record the meeting and documents in the HR file.
- [ ] Do not negotiate a new term, bonus or equity promise unless the authorised approver has approved it in writing.

## 4. Exit date and access revocation

- [ ] Disable email, SSO, source control, cloud, admin, payroll, banking and payment access.
- [ ] Revoke API keys, tokens, certificates and shared secrets.
- [ ] Rotate shared credentials the employee could access.
- [ ] Remove the employee from distribution lists, groups and on-call rotations.
- [ ] Recover laptops, phones, security keys, ID cards, storage media and documents.
- [ ] Confirm the device is wiped or securely re-provisioned.
- [ ] Confirm company data has been transferred and personal copies removed.
- [ ] Update the asset register and access matrix.
- [ ] Record the revocation time and the person who completed it.

## 5. Handover and knowledge transfer

- [ ] Document open projects, owners, deadlines and risks.
- [ ] Transfer source code, design files, research, customer cases and contracts to approved storage.
- [ ] Transfer administrator or approver responsibilities.
- [ ] Update the organisation chart and succession plan.
- [ ] Notify key customers, partners and vendors through the approved channel.
- [ ] Confirm no company data remains in personal email, chat or drives.
- [ ] Record the handover completion.

## 6. Final pay and benefits

- [ ] Calculate salary through the last day.
- [ ] Calculate accrued but unused leave under local law and the contract.
- [ ] Calculate commissions, bonus or other earned compensation under the written plan.
- [ ] Apply lawful deductions and statutory contributions.
- [ ] Confirm pension, insurance and benefit end dates.
- [ ] Confirm equity or option treatment under the plan documents; do not invent a vesting or exercise decision.
- [ ] Issue the final payslip and any required tax document.
- [ ] Confirm the payment date and method.
- [ ] Record the final-pay approval.

## 7. Records and retention

- [ ] Preserve the contract, NDA, IP assignment, acknowledgements, payroll records, leave records, performance records and termination documents.
- [ ] Preserve investigation, grievance or disciplinary records where applicable.
- [ ] Apply the retention schedule and any litigation hold.
- [ ] Restrict access to the separated employee’s file.
- [ ] Delete or de-identify data that is no longer needed, subject to the retention schedule.
- [ ] Record the retention decision and destruction date.

## 8. Exit interview

- [ ] Offer a voluntary exit interview.
- [ ] Explain that the interview is not a negotiation or a condition of final pay.
- [ ] Record themes without unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] Route a misconduct, safety, harassment or security concern to the proper channel.
- [ ] Do not retaliate against the employee for raising a concern.
- [ ] Provide a copy of the exit-interview summary on request where required.

## 9. Post-exit audit

- [ ] Confirm all access is revoked.
- [ ] Confirm all property is returned or the value is recovered under the contract.
- [ ] Confirm final pay and benefits are complete.
- [ ] Confirm records are preserved and restricted.
- [ ] Confirm the manager and team have a coverage plan.
- [ ] Review the exit for a systemic issue and record any remediation.
- [ ] Close the offboarding record.
