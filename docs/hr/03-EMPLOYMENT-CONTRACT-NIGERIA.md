The Company will pay the Employee a gross salary of **NGN [AMOUNT]** per **[MONTH / YEAR]**, payable on or before **[PAY DAY]** each **[PAY PERIOD]** through the approved payroll provider. Payment will be made in **[NGN / APPROVED CURRENCY]** to the Employee’s verified bank account.

## 6. Hours of work and rest

The Employee’s normal hours are **[HOURS]** per week, **[DAYS]**, with the rest breaks required by law. The role may require reasonable additional hours, subject to applicable working-time, rest-period, overtime and health-and-safety rules.

The Employee must record working time accurately where required and must not work unrecorded overtime. The Company will not require work during statutory rest periods or approved leave except in a genuine emergency and only as permitted by law.

## 7. Leave and public holidays

The Employee is entitled to the leave required by Nigerian law, including annual leave, public holidays, sick leave and any other statutory leave, and to any additional leave stated in the Company’s handbook or a local addendum.

Leave must be requested through the approved process. The Employee must provide only the information the Company reasonably needs. Medical information connected with sick leave will be kept confidential.

## 8. Sick leave and incapacity

The Employee must notify the manager as soon as reasonably practicable and follow the Company’s sick-leave procedure. The Company may require reasonable evidence of incapacity where permitted by law. The Company will not retaliate against an employee for taking lawful sick leave.

## 9. Pension and social insurance

The Company will assess whether the Employee and the Company are covered by the contributory pension regime under the Pension Reform Act and will register and contribute where required. The commonly stated reference for covered employees is 10% employer and 8% employee, but applicability and current rates must be confirmed with Nigerian counsel and the relevant regulator.

The Company will also assess NSITF registration and the Employees’ Compensation Scheme contribution and will maintain the workplace-injury and occupational-disease protection required by law.

## 10. Tax

The Company will operate PAYE deductions and remit them to the relevant tax authority. The Employee must provide a valid tax identification number and accurate payroll information and must promptly update the Company when those details change.

## 11. Expenses

The Company will reimburse reasonable, documented business expenses approved in advance under the expense policy. The Employee must provide receipts and a business purpose. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## 12. Confidentiality and intellectual property

The Employee must sign and comply with the Company’s Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement. The Employee must protect confidential information, customer data, source code, research and security information.

The Employee assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in work product created in the course of employment, including code, content, designs, inventions and improvements, to the extent permitted by Nigerian law. The Employee must disclose prior inventions and third-party materials before using them.

## 13. Data protection and privacy

The Company will collect and use employee personal data only for legitimate employment, payroll, tax, pension, insurance, health-and-safety and legal purposes. The Employee must follow the Company’s privacy notice, access controls and retention schedule. The Employee must not copy employee or customer data to a personal device, personal email, chat or unsecured storage.

## 14. Policies and acknowledgements

The Employee must comply with the Employee Handbook, acceptable-use and information-security policy, code of conduct, anti-harassment policy, conflicts-of-interest policy and other lawful policies. The Employee must sign the required acknowledgements.

## 15. Outside activities and conflicts

The Employee must disclose outside employment, consulting, directorships, advisory roles and any financial interest that could conflict with the Company’s interests. The Employee may not engage in an outside activity that competes with the Company, uses Company time or property, or creates a conflict without written approval.

## 16. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving **[NOTICE PERIOD]** written notice, or pay in lieu of notice where permitted by law. The Company may terminate without notice for gross misconduct or another lawful ground.

The Company will follow the notice, consultation, final-pay and documentation requirements of Nigerian law. On termination, the Company will pay salary, accrued leave, lawful allowances and other amounts due, less lawful deductions.

## 17. Return of property

On or before the last day of employment, the Employee must return all Company property, including laptops, phones, security keys, ID cards, documents, credentials and copies of Company data. The Employee must not retain Company data in personal accounts or devices.

## 18. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. The parties will first attempt to resolve a dispute through good-faith discussion. If the dispute is not resolved, it will be referred to **[COURT / ARBITRATION / MEDIATION]** in **[CITY, STATE]**, subject to any mandatory statutory procedure. Nothing in this section limits a non-waivable statutory right.

## 19. Entire agreement and amendments

This Agreement, the Job Description, the Confidentiality and IP Assignment Agreement, the handbook acknowledgements and any applicable plan documents are the entire agreement on their subjects. A change must be in writing and signed by an authorised Company representative.
