# TRADINGBIBLE HOLDINGS, INC. — CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT PACK

**Controlled template · Version 1.0 · 12 November 2026**

> **Controlled template — counsel review required.** This pack contains a confidentiality agreement, a present and future intellectual-property assignment, a prior-inventions schedule, a third-party and open-source schedule and a contractor addendum. It is not an executed agreement. Counsel must review it for the employee’s work country and state, especially employee-invention, moral-rights, non-compete and data-protection rules.

## Part A — Confidentiality and intellectual property agreement

This Agreement is made on **[DATE]** between **TradingBible Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), and **[INDIVIDUAL LEGAL NAME]** (the “Individual”).

The Individual is engaged as **[EMPLOYEE / CONTRACTOR]** in the role of **[ROLE]**, commencing **[START DATE]**.

### 1. Confidential information

“Confidential Information” means information disclosed by or on behalf of the Company, whether oral, written, electronic or visual, that is not generally known to the public or that the Company marks or reasonably treats as confidential. It includes source code, product designs, research, models, data, customer and employee records, financial information, investor materials, contracts, pricing, security information, business plans, trade secrets and information received from a third party under a duty of confidence.

Confidential Information does not include information that:

1. is or becomes public through no breach by the Individual;
2. the Individual can prove was lawfully known before disclosure and without a duty of confidence;
3. is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence; or
4. is independently developed without using Confidential Information.

### 2. Obligations

The Individual will:

- use Confidential Information only to perform authorised work for the Company;
- keep it secure and access it only on a need-to-know basis;
- not disclose it to any person outside the Company without written authorisation;
- not copy it to a personal device, personal email, chat, unapproved AI tool or unsecured storage;
- follow the Company’s information-security, acceptable-use and data-protection policies;
- report a suspected loss, disclosure or unauthorised access immediately; and
- return or destroy all copies when the engagement ends or on request.

The Individual may disclose Confidential Information if legally compelled, provided the Individual gives the Company prompt notice where lawful and cooperates with any protective order. Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Individual from reporting unlawful conduct to a government agency, cooperating with an investigation or exercising a non-waivable statutory right.

### 3. Term

The confidentiality obligations begin on the start date and continue for as long as the information remains confidential. A trade secret remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law. The obligations survive termination.

### 4. Intellectual property assignment

The Individual assigns to the Company all right, title and interest worldwide in all work product created, conceived, developed or reduced to practice by the Individual, alone or with others, in the course of the engagement or using Company resources (the “Work Product”). Work Product includes source code, scripts, models, algorithms, data sets, documentation, designs, content, illustrations, videos, trademarks, inventions, improvements and know-how.

To the extent permitted by law, the Work Product is a “work made for hire” for the Company. If it is not, the assignment in this section applies. The Individual will execute any document and take any reasonable step needed to perfect, record or enforce the Company’s rights.

The Individual waives, to the extent permitted by law, any moral rights or similar rights in the Work Product. Where waiver is not permitted, the Individual agrees not to assert those rights in a way that interferes with the Company’s use.

### 5. Prior inventions and third-party materials

The Individual must complete Schedule 1 (Prior Inventions) before starting. If the Individual does not complete it, the Individual confirms that no prior invention is incorporated into the Work Product.

The Individual must not incorporate third-party code, content, data, images, fonts, models or open-source material into Work Product without written approval and a recorded licence. Schedule 2 must list every third-party or open-source component, its version, licence and intended use.

### 6. No licence or ownership claim

The Individual receives no licence or ownership interest in Confidential Information or Company intellectual property except the limited right to use it for authorised work. The Individual must not use Company intellectual property after the engagement ends.

### 7. Representations

The Individual represents that:

- they have the right to enter this Agreement;
- they are not subject to a conflicting confidentiality, non-compete, invention-assignment or employment obligation;
- they have not brought and will not bring third-party confidential information or materials onto Company systems without authorisation; and
- they will disclose any outside interest that could conflict with the Company’s rights.

### 8. Remedies

The Individual acknowledges that a breach may cause irreparable harm for which money damages are inadequate. The Company may seek injunctive relief and other remedies available at law or in equity. The Company may also recover reasonable legal costs where permitted by law. Nothing in this section limits a non-waivable right.

### 9. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[DELAWARE / WORK STATE / NIGERIA / OTHER]** and applicable law, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. The parties will first attempt good-faith resolution. Any remaining dispute will be resolved in **[COURT / ARBITRATION]** in **[LOCATION]**, subject to mandatory local law.

### 10. Entire agreement and amendments

This Agreement, the employment or contractor agreement, the acceptable-use acknowledgement and any schedules are the entire agreement on their subjects. A change must be in writing and signed by an authorised Company representative.

## Part B — Contractor addendum

This addendum applies when the Individual is engaged as an independent contractor rather than an employee. It does not change the classification test under local law.

1. The Individual is an independent contractor and is not an employee, partner, agent or joint venturer of the Company.
2. The Individual is responsible for their own taxes, insurance, equipment, expenses and statutory contributions unless the contract says otherwise.
3. The Individual must not hold themselves out as an employee or representative of the Company or incur obligations on the Company’s behalf without written authority.
4. The confidentiality, intellectual-property, data-protection, return-of-property and conflict provisions in Part A apply.
5. The Company may terminate the engagement for material breach, insolvency, unlawful conduct or failure to meet agreed deliverables. The contract must state the notice, payment and termination terms.
6. The Individual must provide all deliverables with clear ownership and must disclose any third-party materials before delivery.
7. The Individual must maintain the insurance and licences required for the work and provide evidence on request.
8. The parties must confirm that the engagement does not create an employment relationship under the law of the work location.
