# TradingBible LLC — Global Workforce Strategy & Recruitment Policy
*Worldwide Recruitment · Remote-First · Compliance-First · Proposed — subject to management approval and jurisdiction-specific legal review*

> Source: founder Global Employment, Legal Protection & Corporate Compliance Blueprint (2026–2027 expansion plan). TradingBible LLC (Delaware, USA, managed from Nigeria) is the sole legal entity. No legal structure or contract guarantees freedom from suit — the objective is to prevent avoidable disputes, reduce exposure, maintain insurance, and preserve strong defenses.

## Official hiring model
Keep TradingBible LLC as the existing company. Combine direct employment (where TradingBible can lawfully register, employ, and run payroll) with qualified Employer-of-Record providers (where it wants an employee but has no suitable local entity). Genuine independent contractors for project-based services only — never label an employee a contractor. Form a local subsidiary only where substantial permanent operations justify the cost. PEO co-employment for some U.S. arrangements (does not eliminate employer responsibilities).

Before evaluating any EOR/PEO provider, verify it accepts a Delaware LLC managed from Nigeria, supports the candidate's country, and handles the specific employment requirements. An EOR does not remove all of TradingBible's legal responsibilities.

## The hiring rule (management policy)
**No employee starts work until TradingBible has confirmed:** the worker's actual country and state of work · lawful classification and work authorization · required employer and payroll registrations · compliant compensation, benefits, and insurance · a locally reviewed employment agreement · confidentiality, IP ownership, and security onboarding · an assigned manager and documented approval to hire. Enforced in-product: the hire endpoint rejects (`422`) until the compliance confirmation is complete.

## Hiring process
Advertise without unnecessary nationality restrictions → evaluate skills/experience → conditional offer → verify actual work location → determine arrangement (direct / EOR / contractor / subsidiary / PEO) → complete local compliance (payroll taxes, rights, benefits, authorization, registrations) → sign localized agreements (compensation, duties, confidentiality, IP, statutory protections) → secure onboarding (access, training, expectations).

## Priority order (90 days)
1. **Days 1–7 — audit the two existing arrangements** (one U.S., one Nigeria): state, compensation, dates, classification, payroll-tax handling. Engage U.S. and Nigerian employment/payroll advisers. Correct early — especially any U.S. state where payroll obligations were missed.
2. **Days 8–30** — registrations, wage reconciliation, localized agreements, payroll software, mandatory policies.
3. **Days 31–60** — handbook, security policies, IP assignments, insurance, customer terms, data controls, reporting channels.
4. **Days 61–90** — EOR/direct strategy by country, hiring approval gates, compliance monitoring per location.
5. **Before regulated expansion** — written licensing assessment, authorizations, financial-crime controls, security reviews.

## Protection systems (summary)
- **Corporate:** preserve LLC limited liability, separate finances, registrations, proper contracts, member resolutions, records.
- **Employment:** lawful structure per jurisdiction, correct pay, anti-discrimination/harassment, wage-dispute prevention.
- **Tax:** U.S. federal/state payroll (W-4, I-9, 941, 940, W-2/W-3), Nigerian PAYE/pension/NSITF + 2026 reforms review, foreign-owned LLC Form 5472 + pro forma 1120 where applicable, Delaware $300/yr. Personal ITIN (Form W-7) only if a 1040-NR obligation exists.
- **Product/customer:** data licenses with redistribution rights, accurate disclosures, no regulated brokerage/advisory/custody without authorization, no guaranteed-profit claims, terms/privacy/billing-consent/complaints/retention/MFA/RBAC/encryption/auditlogs/backups/breach procedures.
- **Operational:** EPLI, cyber, tech E&O, workers' comp (state-mandatory), general liability + D&O with U.S.+Nigeria territorial coverage; incident response; legal-response protocol (preserve records, no retaliation, notify insurers, counsel before responding; never delete messages, alter payroll, backdate agreements, or fire whistleblowers).

## Governance
Management reviews this policy periodically and updates local procedures as laws change. *Approved entity: TradingBible LLC. Status: proposed — implement only after qualified U.S. employment counsel, U.S. CPA (foreign-owned LLCs), and Nigerian corporate/employment/tax advisers review.*
