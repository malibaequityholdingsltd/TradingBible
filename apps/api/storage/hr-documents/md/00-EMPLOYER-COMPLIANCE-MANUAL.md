# TRADINGBIBLE HOLDINGS, INC. — EMPLOYER COMPLIANCE MANUAL

**Controlled template · Version 1.0 · 12 November 2026**

> **Controlled template — counsel review and local adoption required.** This document is a company policy or reference framework. It is not an executed agreement, a legal opinion, a regulatory approval, a payroll filing, an insurance policy or a promise of a benefit. Record adoption and approval references in the Workforce Compliance Register before treating it as operative. Local addenda control where they are more protective.

## 1. Purpose and status

This manual is the operating control framework for the company as an employer. It covers the company, directors and managers, full-time and part-time employees, remote employees and individual contractors. It separates a requirement from the evidence that the requirement is complete. A requirement is not complete because a policy file exists; the underlying filing, signed agreement, provider record, insurance certificate or approval must exist.

**Company:** TradingBible Holdings, Inc. (Delaware corporation)

**Planned public launch:** 25 December 2026

**Workforce scope:** Direct employees, full-time and part-time employees, remote employees and individual contractors. Academy learners and instructors are product users, not employees.

**Legal boundary:** A Delaware registration does not satisfy employment, payroll, tax, insurance or workplace obligations in the country or state where a person actually works. The applicable local rules must be confirmed before a start date.

## 2. Workforce model

Do not hire all 58 catalogue roles at once. Start with a controlled core team and expand only when revenue, funding and operational need justify the role. Distinguish appointed people from open positions; never present an open role as an existing executive.

**Priority open leadership roles (not appointments):** Chief Operating Officer · Chief Financial Officer · Chief Technology Officer · Chief Revenue Officer · General Counsel.

The company must maintain one employee and contractor register with legal name, work country and region, arrangement, start date, manager, status and exit date. A hiring catalogue is not an employee register. An open role is not an appointed person.

## 3. Employer obligations

The register is organised into five employer sections. Every item records an owner, an evidence reference, a status and a review date. The status options are: not started, in progress, complete, blocked and not applicable.

## 9. Jurisdiction boundary

### United States

Federal rules apply nationwide, then the state and local rules where the employee actually works. Delaware incorporation does not replace state payroll, withholding, insurance or leave registrations.

**Caution:** State and local rules can be more protective than federal law. Confirm the law of the employee’s actual work location before the start date.

### Nigeria

A US company employing someone who works in Nigeria must establish an appropriate Nigerian employment and payroll arrangement. Delaware incorporation does not remove Nigerian employment, tax, pension, social-insurance or data-protection obligations.

**Caution:** Pension coverage, contribution rates, NSITF contributions and written-particulars requirements depend on the current law and the employment arrangement. Confirm with Nigerian counsel before the first payroll run. The commonly stated pension contribution reference is 10% employer and 8% employee for covered employees; do not treat that as a conclusion for every worker.

### Other countries

Do not use a US or Nigerian template for another country. Engage local employment counsel and a payroll provider before making an offer or setting a start date.

**Caution:** This register does not provide a legal conclusion for any country outside the United States and Nigeria.

## 11. Policy notes

- A policy is not adopted because a file exists. Record the approving body, date and reference before treating it as operative.
- Local law may require a works council, employee representative, translated document, statutory notice or specific language. Confirm before use.
- Do not promise equity, options, bonuses, health insurance or relocation support until the board, plan documents, tax analysis and local law support the promise.
- Never ask an employee to provide irrelevant medical, family, political, religious or financial information.
- Store identity, medical, immigration, payroll and background-check records in the controlled HR file with need-to-know access.
- The platform does not replace employment counsel, a payroll provider, an insurer or a local accountant.

## 12. Adoption and evidence standard

1. Identify the legal entity and the actual work location for every person.
2. Confirm the employment or contractor classification with local counsel.
3. Complete the payroll, tax, pension, insurance and workers-compensation registrations before the first pay run.
4. Obtain signed contracts and agreements before access to company systems or confidential information.
5. Record the evidence reference and approver in the register.
6. Review the register at least quarterly and after a material change in law, location, headcount or business model.
7. Do not mark an item complete because a document exists. Complete means the underlying obligation has been satisfied and the evidence can be produced.
