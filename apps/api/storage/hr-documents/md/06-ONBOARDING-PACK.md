# TRADINGBIBLE HOLDINGS, INC. — ONBOARDING PACK

**Controlled template · Version 1.0 · 12 November 2026**

> **Controlled template — local-law review required.** This pack is an operating procedure and checklist. It is not an employment contract. Complete the local contract, payroll registration, right-to-work check, insurance and data-protection steps before the start date. Do not collect identity, medical, immigration or bank records in this document; use the controlled HR file and the approved payroll channel.

## 1. Before the offer

- [ ] Role approved with a written job description and salary band.
- [ ] Headcount and fully loaded budget approved by the CFO or authorised officer.
- [ ] Work country, state/region and remote-work location confirmed.
- [ ] Employee or contractor classification confirmed under local law.
- [ ] Lawful employing entity or employer-of-record arrangement confirmed.
- [ ] Local contract and mandatory particulars identified.
- [ ] Payroll provider, currency and pay frequency confirmed.
- [ ] Insurance, workers’ compensation, pension and social-insurance obligations checked.
- [ ] Right-to-work and background-check requirements identified.
- [ ] Hiring manager and interview panel assigned.
- [ ] Conflict-of-interest declaration completed by the interviewer where relevant.

## 2. Before the first day

### Documents and agreements

- [ ] Signed local employment contract and any local addendum.
- [ ] Signed confidentiality agreement.
- [ ] Signed intellectual-property assignment and prior-inventions schedule.
- [ ] Signed acceptable-use and information-security acknowledgement.
- [ ] Signed handbook acknowledgement.
- [ ] Identity verified through the controlled HR process.
- [ ] Right-to-work verification completed.
- [ ] Tax and payroll forms completed through the approved provider.
- [ ] Bank/payment details collected through the secure payroll channel.
- [ ] Emergency contact recorded.
- [ ] Qualifications or licence verified where the role requires it.
- [ ] Background check completed where lawful and role-relevant.

### Access and equipment

- [ ] Company email account created.
- [ ] SSO/identity account created with the correct role.
- [ ] Multi-factor authentication enrolled.
- [ ] Laptop or approved device issued and recorded in the asset register.
- [ ] Disk encryption and endpoint protection confirmed.
- [ ] Password-manager access granted.
- [ ] Role-specific software and project-management access granted.
- [ ] Approved storage access granted.
- [ ] ID card or access credential issued where required.
- [ ] Headset, webcam or other equipment issued where required.

### People and payroll

- [ ] Named manager, buddy and escalation route recorded.
- [ ] First-week schedule and first-month objectives prepared.
- [ ] Payroll record created and first pay date confirmed.
- [ ] Pension, insurance and statutory enrolment started where applicable.
- [ ] Health-and-safety and remote-work risk assessment scheduled.
- [ ] Mandatory training assigned.
- [ ] Probation or initial-assessment date recorded only where lawful.

## 3. First day

- [ ] Welcome and company overview.
- [ ] Mission, products, customers and current stage explained without overstating regulatory status.
- [ ] Organisation chart, department remit and reporting line explained.
- [ ] Job description, decision authority and 30/60/90-day objectives reviewed.
- [ ] Security induction: MFA, password manager, phishing, device care, data classification, AI rules and incident reporting.
- [ ] Privacy notice and employee-data handling explained.
- [ ] Health-and-safety induction and emergency procedure completed.
- [ ] Conflicts-of-interest and outside-business declaration completed.
- [ ] Payroll, expenses and leave process explained.
- [ ] Questions answered and contacts recorded.

## 4. First week

- [ ] One-to-one with the manager.
- [ ] Team introductions and key stakeholders.
- [ ] Access check: confirm access is appropriate and no excessive admin rights.
- [ ] Review the job description and confirm the first 30-day priorities.
- [ ] Complete mandatory compliance and security training.
- [ ] Complete the remote-work risk assessment.
- [ ] Confirm the employee file is complete except for items with a documented follow-up date.
- [ ] Confirm the first payroll run will include the employee.
- [ ] Record any accommodation or support request and route it confidentially.

## 5. First 30 days

- [ ] 30-day check-in completed.
- [ ] Objectives reviewed and adjusted if priorities changed.
- [ ] Training completion recorded.
- [ ] Access review completed.
- [ ] Payroll and leave records checked.
- [ ] Any performance, wellbeing or workload concern documented and addressed.
- [ ] Personnel file reviewed for missing items.

## 6. First 60 and 90 days

- [ ] 60-day check-in completed.
- [ ] 90-day review completed against the written objectives.
- [ ] Probation or initial-assessment decision recorded where lawful.
- [ ] Development plan agreed.
- [ ] Compensation and classification reviewed only through the approved process.
- [ ] Any promotion, bonus or equity discussion routed to the board or authorised approver; no promise is made in the review meeting.

## 7. Manager responsibilities

The manager must:

- provide a clear job description, priorities and decision authority;
- hold regular one-to-ones and give specific feedback;
- ensure the employee has the tools, access and training needed;
- monitor workload, wellbeing and working hours;
- record performance concerns promptly and fairly;
- escalate a payroll, access, safety or compliance issue without delay;
- avoid promising a benefit, bonus, equity award or continued employment without written approval;
- complete the probation or initial-assessment review where lawful.

## 8. Employee acknowledgements

The employee confirms that they have:

- received the job description and reporting line;
- received the handbook and applicable local addendum;
- completed the NDA, IP assignment and acceptable-use acknowledgements;
- completed the security and privacy induction;
- completed the health-and-safety induction;
- been told how to request leave, expenses, support and an accommodation;
- been told how to report misconduct, harassment, fraud or a security incident;
- been told who to contact if the manager is unavailable.
