## 3. Compensation

The Company will pay the Employee a base salary of **$[AMOUNT]** per **[YEAR / HOUR]**, less lawful deductions, payable in accordance with the Company’s payroll schedule. The Employee’s classification for overtime purposes is **[EXEMPT / NON-EXEMPT]**. The classification must be confirmed under the law of the work state.

The Employee is eligible for the following additional compensation only if a separate written plan or approval says so: **[BONUS / COMMISSION / NONE]**. The Company does not promise a bonus, commission, equity award, option, token allocation or other incentive unless the board or an authorised officer has approved the plan and the Employee has signed the applicable award or plan document.

## 4. Benefits and leave

The Employee is eligible to participate in the benefit plans and leave arrangements made available to similarly situated employees, subject to the plan documents, eligibility rules and applicable law. The Company may change or end a benefit plan in accordance with its terms and applicable law.

The Employee will receive the leave required by applicable federal, state and local law and any additional leave stated in the Company’s handbook or a local addendum. The Employee must follow the Company’s leave-request procedure and provide only the information the Company reasonably needs.

## 5. Expenses

The Company will reimburse reasonable, documented business expenses approved in advance in accordance with the expense policy. The Employee must provide receipts and a business purpose. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## 6. Policies and acknowledgements

The Employee must comply with the Company’s handbook, acceptable-use and information-security policy, privacy notice, code of conduct, anti-harassment policy, conflicts-of-interest policy and other lawful policies. The Employee must sign the acknowledgements required for the role.

The Employee must not present the Company as a licensed broker, investment adviser, custodian, exchange or fund manager unless the relevant activity is authorised and operational. The Employee must not promise investment returns, guaranteed trading profits, guaranteed funded-account payouts or guaranteed investor returns.

## 7. Confidentiality, intellectual property and data

The Employee must sign and comply with the Company’s Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement. The Employee assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in work product created within the scope of employment, including source code, content, designs, inventions and improvements, to the extent permitted by law. The Employee must disclose prior inventions and third-party materials before using them.

The Employee must protect personal data, customer data, credentials and confidential information. The Employee must not use unapproved AI tools for customer data, source code, credentials or material non-public information.

## 8. Outside activities and conflicts

The Employee must disclose outside employment, consulting, directorships, advisory roles and any financial interest that could conflict with the Company’s interests. The Employee may not engage in an outside activity that competes with the Company, uses Company time or property, or creates a conflict without written approval.

## 9. Work authorization and tax forms

The Employee must provide the documentation required for Form I-9 within the lawful timeframe, complete Form W-4 and applicable state withholding forms, and provide accurate payroll information. The Employee must maintain work authorization for the duration of employment.

## 10. Termination

Employment is at-will unless a written agreement or applicable law provides otherwise. Either party may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to applicable law. The Company will pay final wages, accrued leave and other amounts required by law or the applicable plan.

The Company may place the Employee on paid administrative leave during an investigation where lawful. Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Employee from reporting unlawful conduct to a government agency or participating in a protected investigation.

## 11. Return of property

On or before the last day of employment, the Employee must return all Company property, including laptops, phones, security keys, ID cards, documents, credentials and copies of Company data. The Employee must not retain Company data in personal accounts or devices. The Company may verify that personal copies have been removed.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[WORK STATE]**, without regard to conflict-of-law rules, and applicable federal law. The parties will first attempt to resolve a dispute through good-faith discussion. Any remaining dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in **[COUNTY / DISTRICT]**, unless applicable law requires a different forum or procedure. Nothing in this section limits a non-waivable statutory right.

## 13. Entire agreement and amendments

This Agreement, the Job Description, the Confidentiality and IP Assignment Agreement, the handbook acknowledgements and any applicable benefit plan documents are the entire agreement on their subjects. They replace prior discussions and representations. A change must be in writing and signed by an authorised Company representative. The Employee may not rely on an oral promise about pay, equity, bonus, benefits, promotion or continued employment.

## 14. Severability and survival

If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to the extent permitted by law. The confidentiality, IP, data-protection, return-of-property and dispute provisions survive termination.

## 15. Acknowledgements

The Employee confirms that they have read this Agreement, had an opportunity to ask questions and received a copy. The Employee understands that this Agreement does not promise a fixed term, a bonus, equity, a token allocation or a particular benefit unless a separate written document says so.
