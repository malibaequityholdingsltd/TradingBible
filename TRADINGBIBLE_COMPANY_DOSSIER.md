## 2. What TradingBible is

TradingBible is a financial-technology company and trading-intelligence platform built around one idea: **the trade, the decision and the review should live in one honest record**.

The platform combines:

- a professional trading terminal and charting workspace;
- a broker-synced trade journal;
- performance, risk and discipline analytics;
- SI-generated reviews and coaching;
- signals, indicators, heatmaps and order-flow tools;
- structured education through Academy 2.0;
- funded-evaluation (“prop”) programs;
- a community, webinars and TradingBible TV;
- wallet, billing and developer/API surfaces;
- a public company record, investor-relations pages and read-only document library.

It is **not** a broker-dealer, investment adviser, fiduciary, exchange or custodian. Software and education do not create a licence to provide regulated brokerage, advisory, exchange or custodial services.

### Mission

Empower traders and investors through transparent, data-driven market intelligence, advanced financial technology, trading analytics, risk management and accessible education.

### Vision

Establish TradingBible as a globally recognised financial-technology ecosystem connecting professional trading tools, brokerage infrastructure, market intelligence, digital assets and investment technology.

### Purpose

Develop, own, operate, license and commercialise lawful fintech platforms, software, analytical tools, educational products, data services and related businesses — subject to applicable licences.

### Voice

Professional, precise, research-driven, globally accessible and transparent about financial risk. The public record prefers evidence over marketing.

## 9. Funded evaluations — TradingBible Funded

TradingBible Funded is an evaluation and trader-development product. It is **not** a hedge fund, pooled vehicle or investment in TradingBible Capital.

### Program formats

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| **Two-step evaluation** | Two structured stages: the first proves the process, the second verifies it under the same written rails |
| **One-step evaluation** | One evaluation stage for traders who want a shorter path and accept tighter consistency rules |
| **Dollar-ruled futures** | Futures evaluations sized in dollars, with contract limits and exchange-hours rules stated up front |

### Current catalog position

- **16 products** in the live catalog at the time of the audit.
- **Maximum account size:** $200K.
- **Maximum profit split:** 90%.
- **Payout cycle:** 14 days.
- **One live seat per balance.** A trader may hold multiple balances but not two live attempts at the same balance; rebuy opens only after breach or payout.
- Exact balance, fee, target, drawdown, daily rail, consistency rule and payout terms are shown in the app before purchase.
- The in-app catalog is the source of truth. Public fallback cards do not invent live terms.
- Funded accounts are owned execution/paper accounts inside the platform; no outside broker is in the loop for the evaluation.
- Fees and payouts settle in **TBC credits** at the published rate; TBC credits are an in-app ledger currency, not the proposed TBC token.

### Honest limits

- A pass is not guaranteed.
- Evaluation fees, rules and payout terms apply.
- Funded evaluation is not a brokerage account, deposit or investment product.
- First funded payout may refund the entry fee in TBC credits.
